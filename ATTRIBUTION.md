## Source model

This project adapts **[T 800 low poly (PORT/P3D)](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/t-800-low-poly-portp3d-ce8c971d7b4240229bbebed9b53198b6)**, credited on Sketchfab to **[Jonn (`Sketchfab_apk_gratis`)](https://sketchfab.com/Sketchfab_apk_gratis)** and published there under **[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**. The source account, title, and declared license were checked against Sketchfab's official model metadata on September 25, 2026.

Jonn is credited here as the source uploader/porter. The source title labels the model as a port, and its description says the uploader ported it. The available source notice does not identify an earlier mesh creator or establish permissions for an earlier port source. This attribution preserves the available source credit; it does not claim that earlier authorship or rights have been independently established.

The source model and the adaptations in this project are modified from that source. Modifications include conversion to Jedi Knight/Mysteries of the Sith 3DO and MAT assets, skeleton fitting, body and head proportion adjustments, eye and material work, level-of-detail integration, retail multiplayer packaging, indexed 8-bit material conversion, an optional holding-hand pose made from the original hand segments, and the v1.0.1 leg piston repair. That repair changes only hip and thigh mesh sections, reuniting split piston surfaces and adding seated upper connections. The neck and every other non-leg mesh are restored exactly to the approved v1.0.0 model; experimental neck changes are excluded. Preserve this source credit, license link, and modification notice when redistributing the adapted assets.
